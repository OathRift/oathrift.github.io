Builds

With no fixed classes, your build is the sum of your choices: which attributes you raise, which trees you learn, what you wear and what you put in each hand. Here is how to plan one, and six to start from.

Step by step

Making your own build

Every level gives you attribute points to spend and one tree point. Nothing is permanent: the Tome of Renewal resets attributes and the Tome of Forgetting resets tree points. So experiment.

助

Bank points before level 10. The five tome trees open at level 10. If you already know you want one of them, you can leave tree points unspent until then and pour them in the moment you read the tome.

  1. Pick a role

    Damage from range, damage up close, protecting the party, or keeping it alive. Most builds lean into one and borrow from a second.

  2. Pick your main attribute

    Your weapon decides it: Strength for swords and axes, Dexterity for bows and daggers, Intelligence for spells. Pour most points there.

  3. Pick one or two trees

    Going deep in one tree strengthens all its attacks; a second tree adds tools. Check the tome requirements early so your attributes are ready at level 10.

  4. Match your gear

    Gear asks for a level and an attribute, and the best armour sets also ask for points in a specific tree. Plan for them.

  5. Fill both hands

    Each hand has five slots for skills or potions. Put the skills you cast together on the same hand, and keep a potion on each.

  6. Think about how you play

    In VR, melee rewards real swings and shields need your arm up. On PC, ranged builds and hotbar-heavy kits feel natural.

Six attributes

What each attribute does

Every attribute starts at the same value. Raising an attribute pays off more and more the higher it climbs, so focus beats spreading thin.

STR

Strength

Power of swords, axes and bare fists. Heavy armour, axes and the Heavy Knight tome all ask for it.

AGI

Agility

Adds to your defence. Daggers, light armour and the Assassin tome ask for it.

VIT

Vitality

Your maximum health, and the strength of Life Drain. Every build wants some.

INT

Intelligence

Your maximum mana and the power of most spells. Staves, cloth armour and the Holy and Draining Fang tomes ask for it.

DEX

Dexterity

Power of bows and daggers. Ranged and light armour, and the Assassin and Lady Luck tomes ask for it.

LCK

Luck

Critical hit chance (crits hit harder and pierce armour), rare drop chance, Card Fan's power and Dice Thrust's sixes.

Levelling path

  • Lv 1-9Mana Well, Ice Bolt, Spell Focus, Mana Shield, then Ice Laser.
  • Lv 10-20Arcane Bulwark, Fire Nova and Mana Attunement; rank up Mana Well and Spell Focus.
  • Lv 20+Chain Lightning, then max every passive.

Gear

A staff (it powers up Chain Lightning), Linen into Wanderer or Witch, then Pilgrim cloth. An Intelligence necklace.

How to play it

Open with Mana Shield, keep Ice Bolt flowing, and save Ice Laser for big targets. When a crowd gathers, Flame Burst or the slow but enormous Fire Nova clears it. Chain Lightning is your finisher for packs.

  • Huge area damage
  • Mana Shield soaks punishment
  • Everything from range
  • Long casts can be interrupted by movement
  • Hungry for mana
  • Fragile when the shield drops

Levelling path

  • Lv 1-9Swordsmanship: two ranks of Blade Discipline, then Enhance Strength, Crescent Slash and Enhance Speed. Raise Strength for the tome.
  • Lv 10-20Read the Heavy Knight Tome from Garrick. Shield Bash, Taunt, Bulwark and Fortitude, then Iron Body and Enhance Defense.
  • Lv 20+Taunt All, Enhance Fire, rank up the defensive passives, and reach Last Man Standing.

Gear

Traveler, Blackiron and Knight plate on the way up. Endgame is the Bastion set and the Aegis of Dawn, both reserved for Heavy Knights.

How to play it

Taunt the biggest threat, raise your shield into its blows, and Shield Bash anything that gets too close. Taunt All when a pull goes wrong. Last Man Standing turns one killing blow into a few seconds of invulnerability: that is your window to drink, or to let the healer catch up.

  • Very hard to kill
  • Controls where monsters look
  • Shield Bash grows with defence
  • Lower damage than pure attackers
  • Needs a shield up, literally, in VR
  • Slow to clear alone

Levelling path

  • Lv 1-9Rank Steady Aim as you go, taking Power Shot, Spread Shot and Enhance Chain Lightning as each opens. Raise Dexterity and some Luck.
  • Lv 10-20Max Steady Aim. Save for the Lady Luck Tome, then rank Lucky Charm and take Fortune's Favour.
  • Lv 20+Treasure Hunter for better drops; rank Lucky Charm and Fortune's Favour to the top.

Gear

Ranger or Leather, then Sylvan and Corsair. The Windrunner set opens once you have invested in Archery. Rings add to every arrow.

How to play it

Keep Enhance Chain Lightning running so every arrow jumps to the pack. Prime Spread Shot for groups and Power Shot for big single targets or long range. Luck turns ordinary arrows into crits, and Treasure Hunter makes every kill a little more rewarding.

  • Safe, long range
  • Strong crits
  • Better loot luck
  • Few tools if something reaches you
  • The Lady Luck Tome is rare and costly
  • Drawing every arrow is a workout in VR

Levelling path

  • Lv 1-9Fight with daggers and bank your tree points. Raise Dexterity and Agility, and save coins: the tomes are costly.
  • Lv 10-20Way of the Assassin first: Backstab, Shadow Edge, Shadow Blades, Stealth, Poisoned Daggers, Disengage.
  • Lv 20+Read the Lady Luck Tome: Dice Thrust, Lucky Charm and a few ranks of Loaded Dice, then Card Fan. Later, Lingering Shadow and Quick Shuffle.

Gear

A dagger in each hand. Shadow and Corsair leathers on the way, then the Shadowstalker set, reserved for Assassins. The Twilight Fang can be traded for quest Medals.

How to play it

Slip into Stealth, get behind your target and open with Backstab for a massive hit. Keep both daggers moving to stack poison, weave in Dice Thrust whenever it is ready (its cooldown is tiny, and Luck brings more sixes), and Card Fan the crowd. If it goes wrong, Disengage and walk away.

  • Highest single-target burst
  • Poison ignores armour
  • Escape tools
  • Two rare tomes to afford
  • Fragile in a straight fight
  • Rewards positioning and timing

Levelling path

  • Lv 1-9Magic: two ranks of Mana Well, Ice Bolt, then Mana Shield. Raise Intelligence for the tome.
  • Lv 10-20Read the Holy Tome from Pell. Heal, Devotion, Holy Bolt, Blessed Body, then Cleanse.
  • Lv 20+Heal Area, then rank Devotion, Blessed Body and Mana Well.

Gear

A staff and cloth armour: Linen, Wanderer or Witch, then Pilgrim. Necklaces with Intelligence or Vitality both help.

How to play it

Heal flies along your aim and mends the first ally it touches, so point at the person in trouble. Heal Area when the whole party takes a hit, and Cleanse anyone poisoned by the Flower Dryad or a Withering Devil Tree. Between heals, Holy Bolt has no cooldown at all.

  • Every party wants one
  • Removes poison
  • Mana Shield for safety
  • Modest damage alone
  • Mana management is the job
  • Aim matters for heals

Levelling path

  • Lv 1-9Swordsmanship: two ranks of Blade Discipline, then Enhance Strength, Crescent Slash and Enhance Speed. Put enough into Intelligence for the tome.
  • Lv 10-20Read the Draining Fang Tome from Pell. Life Drain, Sanguine Feast, Night Hunger, Petrify.
  • Lv 20+Vampiric Vigor and Swarm, then rank the passives in both trees.

Gear

A sword, and Blackiron or Knight plate for staying power. Later, the Duelist set if you add Agility.

How to play it

Wade in sword first. Life Drain costs no mana and heals you for what it takes, so cast it whenever you are hurt. Petrify the hardest hitter while you deal with the rest, and Swarm groups lined up in front of you. Vampiric Vigor quietly raises the Strength and Vitality the whole build runs on.

  • Heals without potions
  • Hard control with Petrify
  • Comfortable solo
  • Attributes spread across three stats
  • Lower peak damage
  • Swarm needs mana
