Gear

Weapons, shields, armour sets, jewellery, potions and the crafts that improve them. Every item asks for a level and an attribute, and the finest ask for dedication to a skill tree.

Seven tiers

Rarity

Every piece of equipment rolls a rarity when it drops. Higher rarities have stronger stats, and from Uncommon up, an item's attribute bonuses come alive. Luck and Rift runs improve your odds.

Items can also carry a + level, either from the drop or from fusing. Each + makes the item stronger.

Choose your arms

Weapons and shields

Better weapons need a higher level and more of their attribute. Each one adds bonuses to its main attribute and a second one.

Swords

One-handed. Strength.

The all-rounder. Swordsmanship skills like Crescent Slash need one.

Daggers

One-handed. Dexterity and Agility.

Quick and deadly, best in pairs. Powered by Way of the Assassin and Lady Luck.

Axes

Two-handed. Lots of Strength.

Huge, slow hits with a little extra reach.

Staves

One-handed. Intelligence.

A caster's focus, with bonuses to Intelligence and Vitality. Chain Lightning draws on its power.

Bows

Two-handed. Dexterity.

Long-range power with unlimited arrows, boosted by Archery.

Shields

Off hand.

Raise one to block. Bonuses to Vitality and Strength. The Aegis of Dawn is reserved for Heavy Knights who have learnt Bulwark.

Bare fists work too, in a pinch. Some of the finest blades, like the Knight's Blade and Twilight Fang, are traded for quest Medals rather than bought.

Rings

A ring adds its power to every weapon hit and arrow from that hand. Rings do not boost spells.

Necklaces

Necklaces add Vitality or Intelligence, growing stronger at higher levels. Their bonuses always apply, whatever the rarity.

Drink up

Potions and elixirs

Any consumable can sit in a hand slot, ready to drink mid-fight.

Health potions

Small, Medium, Large and Greater. Sold by Pell and Isolde.

Mana potions

Small, Medium, Large and Greater. Every caster's best friend.

Elixirs

Brewed, never sold. Elixir of Might (Strength), Swiftness Draught (speed), Ironbark Tonic (defence) and Fortune Elixir (Luck).

Alchemy

Gather herbs in the wild, or take them from monsters, and brew potions and elixirs. Alchemy never fails: if you have the ingredients, you get the brew.

Plus Moonleaf, a pale leaf that drinks in moonlight.

Fusing

Fuse an item with a copy of itself to raise its + level. The first steps are safe; the higher you go, the lower the odds. Win or lose, the copy is consumed, so fuse spare drops, not treasures.

Skill tomes

Tomes unlock skill trees or reset your choices. Garrick and Pell sell them, and monsters very rarely drop them.
