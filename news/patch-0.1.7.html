Patch Notes

Version 0.1.7 patch notes

A rebuilt Vraethon, Rifts that overflow onto the land, the Fishing Town and Mountain Outpost brought to life, sea monsters, Episode 4 and emotes.

Patch Notes20 November 2026Version 0.1.7

Highlights

  • Vraethon, where every adventure begins, has been rebuilt, with a grand Cathedral in the Capital and towns that feel lived in.
  • A Rift left uncleared now overflows, and its monsters march on the nearest town until adventurers drive them back.
  • The Fishing Town and the Mountain Outpost are full of people, with healers, shops, quests and guards who fight for their homes.
  • Crabs and octopuses have come ashore on the beaches around the Fishing Town.
  • The story continues in Episode 4, and you can now share how you feel with the new emote wheel.

New

  • A rebuilt Vraethon: the human continent has been rebuilt, and its townsfolk, monsters and village animals have all moved in. Explore Vraethon.
  • The Cathedral of the Dawn: the Capital's great cathedral now stands at the heart of the city.
  • Lived-in towns: the towns of Vraethon are busier and more detailed, full of the bustle of everyday life.
  • Town moods: towns and districts now have their own light and colour, with a soft anime glow over the land.
  • Rift overflows: when nobody clears a Rift for too long, its monsters, Rift lord and all, spill out through the portal and march on the nearest town. Townsfolk cower and flee, and a world quest calls every adventurer to help. Everyone who joins in is rewarded for their share. How overflows work.
  • Rift cataclysms: leave an overflow to rage and it can grow into a cataclysm that makes every monster on Vraethon bigger, angrier and far more dangerous, until the Rift lord falls.
  • The Fishing Town and the Mountain Outpost: both towns are home to townsfolk with their own daily routines, a healer, a shop and new quests. Meet the townsfolk.
  • Town guards: guards stand watch over the Fishing Town, the Mountain Outpost and Forest Hamlet. When monsters come too close they draw their swords and fight them off.
  • Sea monsters: Red Crabs, Blue Crabs, Octopuses and the formidable Octofem roam the beaches around the Fishing Town, for adventurers from level 32 to 40. They drop three new crafting materials: Crab Shell, Octopus Ink and Sea Pearl. See the sea monsters.
  • Episode 4, Where the Fish Lay: the main story continues, and takes you to the Fishing Town. The episodes.
  • Emotes: hold the left menu button in VR or G in Computer mode to open the emote wheel, pick a feeling, and a chibi bubble pops up over your head for everyone nearby to see.
  • Tails: tails now sway as you move and stream out behind you when you run.

Improvements

  • Monster threat: monsters now go after whoever is the biggest threat. Hitting hard, holding a shield up and healing your friends all draw attention, so a tank can keep monsters off the rest of the party. Monsters far below your level back away instead of picking a fight.
  • Seeing through foliage: leaves and bushes between you and the camera now fade away, and monsters and townsfolk hidden behind foliage glow through it.
  • Character look: characters have a richer anime finish.
  • Sound: sound effects across the game have been reworked.
  • Animation: smoother blending between moves, with more natural twist through the hips and spine.
  • Scatter: the Scatter archery skill has been improved.
  • Rivers: Vraethon's rivers have been reshaped.
  • Monster spawns: monsters have been rearranged across Vraethon.
  • Performance: the game runs more smoothly, especially in detailed areas with a lot to see.

Fixes

  • Fixed Celestial and Demon wings flapping incorrectly.
  • Fixed running animations.
  • Fixed problems with cliffs.
