Version 0.1.6: the Goblin Warrens open

A second Rift, the Axeman skill tree, a new crafting system and a story told in episodes. Here is what is coming on 6 November.

Announcement5 November 2026Version 0.1.6

Version 0.1.6 lands on 6 November at 7am AWST. The launcher installs the patch for you, so there is nothing to download by hand. Here is what to look forward to. The full list arrives in the patch notes with the release.

A second Rift: the Goblin Warrens

A new portal has opened just east of the Mountain Outpost. Beyond it, a warren of mines and caverns winds deep underground, lit only by ore veins and crystal, and every goblin inside is an Elite. At the bottom waits the Goblin General. This is a level 50 challenge for a full party, and we would rather you found out how he fights for yourselves. Read the Rift profile.

The Axeman

A ninth skill tree for anyone who likes their problems cut in half. Read the Axeman Tome to learn wide sweeps, crushing blows and a spin that clears a crowd. Every other tree gains something new as well, from Revive for healers to a decoy clone for gamblers. See the skills.

Crafting, rebuilt

Alchemy is joined by Cooking, Blacksmithing, Woodcraft and Tailoring, each with its own level to raise. Collect recipe books, learn from crafting masters in the Capital, gather ore, wood and flax in the wild, and make gear of your own that rolls its own rarity. How crafting works.

A story told in episodes

New characters now begin with a fully animated opening, told by the High Seraphim. From there, the main story plays out like an anime series, in episodes with their own title cards, recaps when you return and story cutscenes along the way. Meet the episodes.

More reasons to keep swinging

  • Weapon mastery: every weapon type ranks up as you use it and earns you titles to wear.
  • Kill counter: chain your kills and watch the count climb.
  • Skill cut-ins and loot pillars: your biggest moments now look the part.
  • Bonds: grow closer to the people at the heart of the story, and hear what they say to a friend.
  • Way-finder: pillars of light on the world itself lead you to where you need to go.
  • Speech-to-text chat: hold to talk and your words appear as chat text.

A better-looking world

Character creation gains faces, eye styles, beards, scars, tattoos and more. Characters move better too, with new weapon stances, smoother animation and cloth that reacts as you do. On the Island, mist settles in the valleys, dust storms roll across the land, plants bend as you pass, and you can see much further. Computer mode has had a lot of attention, with better combat animation and controls.
