Patch Notes

Version 0.1.6 patch notes

The Goblin Warrens, the Axeman skill tree, rebuilt crafting, a story told in episodes and a big pass on animation and Computer mode.

Patch Notes6 November 2026Version 0.1.6

Highlights

  • A second Rift, the Goblin Warrens, is open to level 50 parties.
  • The Axeman joins the skill trees, and other trees gain new skills too.
  • Crafting has been rebuilt from the ground up.
  • The main story now plays out in episodes, starting with a fully voiced opening.
  • Characters move far better, especially in Computer mode.

New

  • The Goblin Warrens: a new Rift of mines and caverns where every goblin is an Elite, and the Goblin General waits at the bottom. Read the Rift profile.
  • Rift entry: a Rift you cannot enter yet tells you why, and an open one asks you to confirm before you step through, so nobody wanders in by accident.
  • Axeman skill tree: wide sweeps, crushing blows and a spin that clears a crowd, plus new skills across the other trees. See the skills.
  • Crafting overhaul: Cooking, Blacksmithing, Woodcraft and Tailoring join Alchemy, each with its own level, recipe books and masters in the Capital. Gear you make rolls its own rarity. How crafting works.
  • Story episodes: the main story is split into episodes with their own title cards and a recap when you return. Meet the episodes.
  • Opening cutscene: new characters begin with a fully animated, voiced opening told by the High Seraphim.
  • Story cutscenes: new scenes along the way, including A Scholar's Eye and your first meeting with Oda.
  • Titles: titles are now worth earning, and you can wear the one you like best.
  • Weapon mastery: every weapon type ranks up as you use it and earns you titles.
  • Kill counter: chain your kills and watch the count climb.
  • Skill cut-ins and loot pillars: your biggest moments and best drops now look the part.
  • Bonds: grow closer to the people at the heart of the story.
  • Way-finder: pillars of light on the world itself lead you to where you need to go.
  • Village animals: chickens, chicks, pigs, rabbits, cats and dogs now roam the Capital and the forest hamlet, chasing and fleeing each other, and the townsfolk stop to fuss over them.
  • Speech-to-text chat: hold to talk and your words appear as chat text.
  • Character creation: more faces, eye styles, beards and other ways to make your character your own.
  • Cloth: capes, skirts and loose clothing on players and NPCs now move with you.
  • Respawn arrivals: monsters return through a tear in the world, and village animals with a soft puff, instead of popping into view.
  • Soul orb: a soul orb now appears when you fall.

Improvements

  • Animation: a full overhaul with smoother blending between moves.
  • Computer mode: better sword and shield, battle axe, bow and dual wield animations, combo attacks, freer leg movement and a range of control improvements.
  • Monster attacks: a gleam sweeps over a monster as its attack is about to land, so it is easier to time your dodge.
  • Monster packs: monsters now spread out and surround you instead of waiting in line, so a big pull is a real threat.
  • The Island: mist settles in the valleys and dust storms roll across the land.
  • A better-looking world: richer grass, terrain and scenery, a more finished art style, and distant landscape that looks much better as you see further.
  • Busy areas: large crowds of players and NPCs now run more smoothly.

Fixes

  • Fixed a bug in character creation.
  • Fixed elf ears and the Bastion armour set.
  • Fixed the fox eyes face option.
  • Fixed talking to NPCs in Computer mode.
  • Fixed windows resizing incorrectly when showing items.
  • Fixed trees that looked wrong at a distance.
