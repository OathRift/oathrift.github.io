Why Oath Rift

An MMO you play with your whole body

Oath Rift Online was built for virtual reality first, then opened up to everyone at a desk. Here is what sets it apart.

剣

Combat that is really yours

No auto-swing in VR. How fast you swing decides how hard you hit, you block by raising your shield into the blow, and you nock, draw and loose every arrow yourself. Monsters telegraph their attacks on the ground, so stepping out of the way is a real skill.

界

One world, three ways to play

Play standalone on Meta Quest, over PC VR, or on a regular PC with keyboard and mouse. Switch between headset and desktop on the fly and keep playing with the same character in the same world.

道

No classes, only paths

There is no class to pick at the start. Eight skill trees, from Magic and Swordsmanship to Way of the Assassin and Lady Luck, can be mixed into the hero you want. Mistakes are fixable: tomes let you reset and rebuild.

誓

An isekai story worth an oath

A tragic Demon Lord, a world split from antimatter, tears in spacetime called Rifts, and a Celestial who called you here and swore to bring you home. The story opens in a hand-inked manga prologue.

裂

Rift raids for your party

Step through a Rift into the Demon Realm with up to five friends. Each party gets its own run through a glowing night forest, with no respawning monsters and a multi-phase boss guarding the only way out.

街

A living, forgiving world

More than sixty townsfolk with their own routines, a royal capital, an auction house, face-to-face trading, herb gathering, alchemy and fusing. Falling in battle costs you nothing but a moment of pride.

Quick answers

New to Oath Rift?

Do I need a VR headset?
No. Oath Rift Online runs in VR on Meta Quest and PC VR, and in a third-person Computer mode on PC with keyboard and mouse. Without a headset the game simply starts in Computer mode.
Which class should I pick?
There are no classes. Every adventurer starts with Magic, Swordsmanship and Archery, and can unlock five more trees from tomes. See Builds for ideas.
What happens when I die?
You are knocked out, wait a short moment and return to the map's spawn point with full health. You keep your items and experience. Fall inside a Rift and you return to the start of that same Rift run.
Is there PvP?
No. Oath Rift Online is cooperative: adventurers party up, trade and bid against each other at the auction house, but never fight each other.
