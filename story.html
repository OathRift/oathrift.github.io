The prologue

The Story

Every adventurer hears this tale on their first night in the world, told in the voice of the Celestial who brought them here. Read it as it appears in game: a hand-inked manga, read right to left within each row.

CreationThe BetrayalExileThe RiftsThe SummoningKey figuresRealmsPeoples

Chapter I

Creation

Before anything was, there was the Eternal One.The High Seraphim

From matter, the Eternal One shaped the heavens and the earth. But matter alone was unstable, and the world trembled. So the Eternal One drew the antimatter apart and formed a second realm to hold the first in balance: the Demon Realm.

The Eternal One is never seen, only felt: as light, and as hands of light shaping the world.

Chapter II

The Betrayal

A thousand years ago, the throne of the Demon Realm belonged to the Demon Lord. His generals were his closest friends. They feasted at his table and laughed at his side.

And one night, while his family slept, they came with blades drawn. His family. His clan. All of them. Only he survived.The High Seraphim

Chapter III

Refuge and Exile

He fled into our world, and the elders of every race gave him refuge. But the darkness came with him. Where he walked, his miasma spread, and the people fell sick.

So the elders banished him to the empty continent in the west. No one has seen him since.The High Seraphim

Was the Demon Lord a monster, or the last survivor of a massacre who was cast out twice? The world has not decided. Neither has he.

Chapter IV

The Rifts

Soon after, the Celestials felt the balance between the realms begin to break.

We found tears in spacetime itself. We named them Rifts.The High Seraphim

For centuries, the Rifts were silent. Then, a few decades ago, the monsters came, and the armies of every kingdom could not stop them. Today a Rift bleeds mana into the plains south of the Capital, and the beasts grow bolder each season.

Chapter V

The Summoning and the Oath

So the Celestials reached beyond the world, and called for help.

We called you.The High Seraphim

You wake in a summoning circle in the Cathedral of the Dawn. The High Seraphim kneels before you, her sword driven into the floor. She is sorry. Your whole life, torn away. But here, you can change the fate of an entire world.

Help us, and I swear I will do everything in my power to bring you home.The High Seraphim

The mana mystery

The monsters of the Island are carrying mana no beast should hold, and the cores they leave behind all pulse to the same, quickening beat. Following that beat is the heart of the main quest line. We will not spoil where it leads.

Cosmology

The three realms

天

Celestial

Bright, cloudy floating islands bathed in gold and white. The Celestial host watch the balance between realms from here, and they were first to find the Rifts.

地

Normal

The world shaped from matter: the lands of humans, demihumans and elves, lit by natural daylight. The Island and the elven continent of Elvenwood lie here.

魔

Demon

The realm drawn from antimatter. Dark, lit only by glowing orbs and plants, heavy with miasma. Every Rift opens onto it, and its monsters pour through.

Character creation

Peoples of the world

Choose any of five peoples when you create your character. Your people shapes how you look, never how strong you are, so pick the one you love.

Human

Found in every town on the Island, from the royal Capital to the fishing village on the bay.

Elf

The people of Elvenwood, the elven continent of moonwells, tree homes and crystal lanterns.

Demihuman

Folk with the ears and tail of an animal, choose the look that suits you.

Demon

Horned, like the Demon Lord himself. A bold choice in a world that remembers him.

Divine

The people of the Celestial host, the same race as the High Seraphim.
