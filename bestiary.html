Field bossLv 15Southern Plains

Shadow Planta

Each Planta camp on the plains is ruled by a Shadow Planta, surrounded by six Planta Slaves. It begins merely curious, lashing out with shadowy vines and bursts of gloom only when provoked.

  • CalmWhile its Slaves still stand, it waits in the camp and only fights if attacked.
  • EnragedCut down enough of its Slaves and it turns Hunting: it leaves the camp and comes for you.

Always drops: a Shadow Petal, wanted for a quest.
Tip: pull the Slaves one at a time and be ready for the boss to come looking once the camp thins out.

Rift bossLv 20AntiForest

The Flower Dryad

The mistress of the AntiForest waits in an arena at the end of the Rift. Her kit mixes a thrashing crescent sweep that hurls you back, a spinning ring of petals, and a long-reaching grass whip. She shrugs off stuns more easily than most.

  • Phase 1Melee-heavy and patient. Her special attacks come slowly: learn her tells here.
  • Phase 2She favours Poison Nova, scattering clouds of poison across the arena. Keep moving and bring a Cleanse.
  • Phase 3Berserk. Everything comes faster. Finish her before she finishes you.

Rewards: a guaranteed haul of high-rarity loot, a Rift Lord mana core, and the key to the exit portal.

Survive

Combat tips

Read the ground

Every monster attack paints its area on the ground before it lands. Step out of the marked zone and the attack misses. Big attacks wind up longer, which is your cue to move.

Break the wind-up

Hit a monster hard enough while it is winding up and you stagger it, cancelling the attack and leaving it open for a moment. Some bosses resist it.

Strike the weak point

A hit on a monster's weak point while it is winding up or staggered is a guaranteed critical hit. In VR, aim your swing; on PC, time it.

Mind your level

Monsters well below your level give less and less experience, until they give none. Move on to the next area when kills stop paying.

Carry a cure

Poison from Withering Devil Trees and the Flower Dryad keeps ticking. A Holy user's Cleanse removes it; potions buy you time.

Go together

Party up for the Rift. Kills shared with nearby party members split experience and coins, and loot goes to a random member, so nobody fights over drops.
