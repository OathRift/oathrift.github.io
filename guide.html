Your first hour

Getting started

始

Latest client

Download Oath Rift Online

Get the newest version of the game from our itch.io page, then follow the steps below to create your account and step into the world.

Download on itch.io

  1. Download the client

    Grab the latest version from blankfoxgirl.itch.io/oath-rift. When a new version comes out, download it from the same page.

  2. Create your account

    Pick a realm (a game server near you) and register with a username and password. Your device remembers you, so next time you go straight in. Signing in on a new device moves your session there.

  3. Create your character

    Choose your people (Human, Elf, Demihuman, Demon or Divine), then your hair, colours and face. Your people is purely cosmetic, so pick what you love. Every character uses the same account bank.

  4. Watch the prologue

    The High Seraphim tells you why you were summoned. You can also read it on the Story page.

  5. Meet Hana in Forest Hamlet

    Hana waits at the west gate with a short tutorial: a body scan to fit your arms in VR, the menu, moving, jumping and dashing, and switching play modes.

  6. Talk to everyone

    Villagers hand out your first quests. Seren, the Mana Scholar, starts the main story. Garrick and Pell sell your first upgrades.

  7. Hunt your first Turnipa

    Walk into the fields outside town. Turnipas only fight back once hit, so they are the perfect first target. Then follow the suggested route.

Three ways to play

Play modes

Meta Quest

Standalone VR, no PC needed. Physical combat with your hands, and graphics options tuned for the headset.

PC VR

The same VR experience streamed from your PC over Link or SteamVR, with the extra headroom of desktop hardware.

Computer mode

Third person with keyboard and mouse, in the style of classic PC MMOs. Switch to it live from VR, or just start the game without a headset.

All three play in the same world, so friends on a headset and friends at a desk can party together.

Casting in VR

In attack mode, each hand has a dial of five skill slots. Hold the grip and roll your wrist to turn the dial, then hold the trigger to charge and release to cast. Glance at the back of a hand to see its cooldowns.

Your body is the controller

Swing your weapon to hit, raise your shield between your head and the attacker to block, and bring your hands together to nock and draw your bow. Turn your left palm toward you for a quick map. In water, look down to dive.

Play your way

Comfort and accessibility

VR comfort is personal. Oath Rift Online lets you tune how you move and what you see, under Menu, Options.

New to VR? Start with teleport, snap turn and a strong vignette, then relax them as you find your legs.

  • MovementSmooth movement or teleport, with a choice of walking speeds
  • TurningSnap turn at several angles, or smooth turn at the speed you like
  • VignetteOff, Low, Medium or High, while moving, turning or both
  • FeelHaptics strength, and speed lines on or off
  • BodyBody scan for your arm length, and hand twist
  • GraphicsRender scale, foveation, space warp and view distance

Steel and spells

How combat works

Press Y in VR or R on PC to enter attack mode, then fight with whatever is in your hands.

Swords, axes and daggers

In VR, every hit is a real swing. A faster swing hits harder, but spamming does not help: hits have a short rhythm. Axes reach a little further. Dual wield two one-handed weapons for extra damage with the right passives.

Shields

Hold your shield up between your head and the attacker and the blow is blocked. Heavy Knights turn their shield into a weapon with Shield Bash.

Bows

Bring your other hand to the bow, grip to nock, pull back and release. Arrows never run out. Archery skills prime your next arrow for something special.

Spells

Turn the dial on a hand to choose a skill, hold the trigger to charge it, and release to cast. Projectiles fly where you point, areas land where you aim, and arcs sweep the space in front of you.

Telegraphs

Monster attacks show their area on the ground before they land. Step out and they miss. Big hits wind up longer.

Stagger and weak points

Hit hard during a wind-up to stagger a monster and cancel its attack. Strike its weak point while it winds up or reels and the hit is a guaranteed critical.

Grow stronger

Progression

Levels and attributes

Defeat monsters and finish quests to earn experience, all the way to level 100. Each level refills your health and mana and gives you attribute points and a skill tree point.

Monsters far below your level give less and less experience. Read more in Attributes and Skill Trees.

Skills and gear

Skills grow stronger with use, up to mastery level 10. Gear comes in seven rarities and can be fused to higher + levels.

See Gear for rarities, armour sets, fusing and alchemy.

Health and mana

Vitality sets your maximum health and Intelligence your maximum mana. Both trickle back on their own, and potions, healers and Holy skills speed things up.

Falling in battle

At zero health your body shatters and you get a short countdown before you respawn at the map's spawn point with full health. You lose nothing: no items, no experience. Fall inside a Rift and you rise again at the Rift's start, still in your run.

Stronger together

Playing together

Parties

Form a party of up to six. Members fighting near each other share experience and coins from every kill, and each dropped item goes to a random member.

Rifts

Walk into the Rift portal on the southern plains. Your party gets its own private run of the AntiForest; the way out opens only when its boss falls. Rift details

Friends and chat

Add friends to find them again, and chat with other adventurers. Press Enter to chat on PC.

Quests and escorts

Townsfolk offer quests from simple errands to escorts, where you protect a traveller on the road. Stay close: an escort fails if they fall or you wander off.

Trading

Trade items face to face with another adventurer, with several offers on each side.

No PvP

Oath Rift Online is cooperative. The only competition between adventurers is at the auction house.

Coin and craft

Economy and crafting

Coins

Copper, silver and gold, a hundred of each to the next. Monsters drop coins, and shops buy your spare loot for part of its value.

Quest currencies

Gems, Valor, Medals and Crests are earned through quests and exchanges, and spent with Garrick and Pell on fixed rewards, including some of the best weapons. They cannot be traded.

Auction house

List items with Marlo in Forest Hamlet for others to bid on. A listing runs for a week, takes a small deposit, and the house keeps a cut of the sale.

Bag and bank

Your bag holds what you carry. The bank, at a chest in every town, is shared by every character on your account, coins included.

Gathering and alchemy

Pick herbs in the wild with X or right click, and collect more from monsters. Brew them into potions and elixirs that always succeed.

Fusing

Fuse an item with a copy of itself to raise its + level. The higher you climb, the riskier it gets, and the copy is always consumed.

Questions

FAQ

Do I need a VR headset?
No. Without a headset the game starts in Computer mode with keyboard and mouse. With one, you can switch between VR and Computer mode while you play.
Which headsets are supported?
Meta Quest standalone, and PC VR headsets over Link or SteamVR.
Can VR and PC players play together?
Yes. Everyone shares the same world, parties, trading and auction house, whatever mode they play in.
Is there a class system?
No. Everyone starts with Magic, Swordsmanship and Archery, and can unlock Heavy Knight, Holy, Draining Fang, Way of the Assassin and Lady Luck with tomes. Mix them freely. See Builds.
I made a mistake with my points. Can I fix it?
Yes. The Tome of Forgetting refunds your skill tree points and the Tome of Renewal refunds your attribute points. Pell in Forest Hamlet sells both.
Does my race matter?
Only for looks. Every people plays the same, so pick the one you like.
What do I lose when I die?
Nothing. You respawn after a short countdown with full health and keep your items and experience.
I get motion sick in VR. What should I change?
Switch to teleport movement and snap turning, and set the comfort vignette to High in Menu, Options. You can relax these later.
Can I cast spells with hand tracking?
Not yet. Casting uses controllers today.
Is there PvP?
No. Oath Rift Online is a cooperative game.
