Skill codex

Skill Trees

There are no classes in Oath Rift Online. Your skills come from eight trees, and you can mix them however you like. Pick a tree below, then click any node to read what it does.

How trees workThe eight treesSkill mastery

The basics

How skill trees work

One point per level

Every level after the first gives you one tree point. Spend it in any tree you have unlocked, or bank it for later: unspent points wait for you.

Gates and roots

Each tree starts from its root nodes. Deeper nodes open once you have learnt the node they grow from and spent enough points in that tree. Nodes below are marked Root, Early, Mid or Deep.

Skills and passives

Square nodes teach a skill you place in a hand slot. Round nodes are passives: always on, and most can be ranked up several times.

Depth pays

Every point you spend in a tree also strengthens that tree's attacks, so going deep in one tree is never wasted, even on passives.

Unlock with tomes

Magic, Swordsmanship and Archery are open from the start. The other five are unlocked by reading a tome, bought in Forest Hamlet or found as a rare drop, more often inside the Rift.

Change your mind

The Tome of Forgetting refunds every tree point (unlocked trees stay unlocked). The Tome of Renewal refunds your attribute points. Pell sells both.

The eight trees

Choose a tree

Magic

Raw elemental spellcraft: fire, ice and lightning, guarded by a shield of pure mana.

Scales with Intelligence

Unlock: Known from the start. Every point you spend in Magic strengthens all your spells.

Fireball

ProjectileKnown by all

A slow-forming fireball with long range. Known by every adventurer.

Flame Burst

AreaKnown by all

Fire erupts in a circle at the spot you target. Known by every adventurer.

Mana Well

PassiveMulti-rank

Each rank deepens your mana pool.

Root node, available as soon as the tree is unlocked.

Ice Bolt

Projectile

A quick, cheap shard of ice on a short cooldown. The spell you will cast most.

Grows from Mana Well. Early in the tree.

Mana Shield

SelfBuff

For a long while, damage drains your mana instead of your health, and your Intelligence hardens your defence.

Grows from Mana Well. Mid in the tree.

Spell Focus

PassiveMulti-rank

Each rank sharpens the damage of all your spells.

Grows from Mana Well. Early in the tree.

Ice Laser

Projectile

Charge, then fire a near-instant beam of ice that hits very hard.

Grows from Ice Bolt. Mid in the tree.

Arcane Bulwark

PassiveMulti-rank

While Mana Shield is up, your Intelligence adds even more to your defence.

Grows from Mana Shield. Mid in the tree.

PassiveMulti-rank

Archery

Patience and precision. Prime a special shot, then draw and release it yourself.

Practice makes perfect

Skill mastery

Skills improve the more you use them. Every cast or landed hit earns a skill experience toward the next of ten mastery levels. Each level makes the skill a little cheaper, faster to cast, quicker to come back and stronger.

Hand slots

Each hand holds five slots: ten in total. Put a skill or a potion in each. In VR, hold the grip and roll your wrist to turn that hand's dial, then hold the trigger to charge and release to cast. On PC, keys 1 to 5 fire the left hand and 6 to 0 the right. Cooldowns are shared between both hands.
